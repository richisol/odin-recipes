Norwegian Tacos

Description

A typical norwegian "Friday Taco" with seasoned minced meat, corn, cheese, guacamole, salsa, and sour cream. Taco is originally a Mexican dish, but Norwegians have made their own version. For many, Friday isn't complete without tacos and everyone often adds their own twist.

Ingredients

  • 8 taco shells or tortillas
  • 400 g minced meat (ground beef) or lean minced beef
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • 200 g tomatoes
  • 150 g sour cream
  • 250 g grated cheese
  • 50 g lettuce
  • 0.5 cucumber, diced
  • 1 red onion, diced
  • 1 can sweetcorn
  • 4 tbsp jalapenos
  • 0.5 jar taco sauce

Steps

  1. Cook the meat Fry the minced meat in a pan. Add taco seasoning according to the package instructions. Let it simmer on low heat while you prepare the vegetables. Add a little water if it becomes too dry.
  2. Make guacamole Dice tomato and cucumber, finely chop onion, and tear lettuce into pieces. Drain the corn. Place everything in seperate bowls. Grate the cheese and put it in a bowl as well.
  3. Warm the tortillas Heat tortillas quickly in a dry pan, microwave, or oven. Keep them warm in foil.
  4. Serve!Put all the ingredients on the table and let everyone assemble their own tacos. You can use soft tortillas, whole wheat versions, taco shells, or tubs.
